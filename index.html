CALIFORNIA COMFORT GRILL

Grown twenty minutes from the kitchen door.

Morgan's On Main sits on a quiet corner of downtown Woodland, in the heart of Yolo County's farmland. The kitchen builds its menu around what the valley grows: stone fruit in summer, brassicas in winter, and a rotation of produce pulled from farms most diners drive past on the way in. The food is unfussy on purpose — comfort food built from ingredients that don't need much help.
